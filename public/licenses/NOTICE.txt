and the performer’s right. Only recordings whose uploader plausibly held both

12. CONTENT SOURCES CALLED AS AN API
============================================================================

The Qur'an text and translations, the recitation audio, the tafsir and the
hadith collections shown in Salati are requested by the user's device directly
from the respective provider (alquran.cloud, quran.com / QuranicAudio,
mp3quran.net, HadeethEnc.com, fawazahmed0/hadith-api and AhmedBaset/hadith-json
via jsDelivr, AlAdhan, Overpass, Nominatim, gold-api.com, Frankfurter).
Salati does not redistribute those works, so no licence copy is required from
us for them; the providers and their terms are named in the app under
"Sources & Licences".

One exception is the German Qur'an corpus used by the on-device assistant: it
contains the Bubenheim & Elyas translation published by the King Fahd Complex
and is served from Salati's own storage for offline search. It is reproduced
unchanged and with attribution, in line with the Complex's terms for
non-commercial distribution of its translations.


If you believe an attribution is missing or wrong, write to
info@menucloud-berlin.de and it will be corrected or the content removed.
